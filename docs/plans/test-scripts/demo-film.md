## How to approve spend, step by step (against the $500 house cap)
1. **Read the price on the button** before you press anything: `N cr`, `up to N cr` or `free`. Hovering shows its dollar value at the public price of a credit.
2. **Say it out loud** to the Producer: the step, the price, the running total so far.
3. **A plan** (Agent mode shows a plan card, "Render N shots · N cr · Approve") is approved once: one tap approves its listed steps at their listed prices, up to its total, including a stated fix allowance of at most two fixes per shot. A new shot, a fix past the allowance or another engine asks again.
4. **Outside a plan every paid step asks**, unless the workspace allows spending without asking (Auto): in Auto, a draft at or under the per-job line may run without a tap, and a job above US$20 (200 cr) never does. Keep the workspace on **Ask** for the film; only an admin changes it.
5. **The balance is checked against a plan's total before it starts**; if it falls short you are offered Top up then (a person's action, an admin's), never halfway through.
6. **A failed step that cost nothing says "Nothing billed"** and offers Retry under the same approval. A failed step that was charged says what it cost: write it on the tally and tell the Producer.
7. **The cap:** $500 a month of engine cost (a $ figure, not credits), with a warning at 80% (once the cap change is deployed; UNKNOWN how it looks in the new design). At the warning, **stop and tell the owner**. The team may see engine dollars (owner, 16 Oct 10:55).
8. **Keep a tally** (a shared sheet): time, step, engine, price shown, charge, running total, who approved. The Producer checks it against the workspace's usage at the end of each day.
9. **Draft before hero:** make draft takes first and hero takes only on shots the Director has chosen (the product default; Draft to cheaper engines, hero to Seedance or Veo or Kling).
Sample prices from the rate card (samples, not quotes): Seedance 2.5 5 s 1080p 43 cr; Kling 3.0 Pro 5 s 1080p with audio 13 cr; a standard still 1 cr; a keyframe still 3 cr; lip-sync sync.so 4 to 10 cr for 5 s (plan samples). A 30 s ad of eight hero shots is therefore in the hundreds of credits before fixes and the Hindi version (UNKNOWN the real total).
