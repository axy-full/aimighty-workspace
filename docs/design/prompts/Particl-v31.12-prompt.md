You are making Particl Prototype v31.12, a revision of v31.11, the build in this window. Start from it and keep everything not named here exactly as it is. Read everything below before you change anything. Where this brief and v31.11 disagree, this brief wins.

RULES (these replace the rules of v31.7; everything in them not contradicted here still holds)
1. Atomik asks one question at a time. Never a card with two or three questions on it. One question, its answers as pills (at most four) and a field; the person answers; the mark in the bar spins while Atomik thinks; the next question comes, as many as Atomik needs, then the quote. No counter ("2 of 5"), no list of what is still to be asked.
2. Atomik never asks at random. Before a thing is made, it asks what it needs to make it; after a thing is made, it studies the result and suggests from what it sees ("The label is soft at this size · Fix the label · 2 cr"). The study is part of the job: a job shows Thinking · Making · Reviewing as three ticks on its card, at one price, quoted before it starts. The question set differs by kind: a face, a product, an environment, a world, a frame, a take and a mix each ask their own questions.
3. Copy is short, technical and single-worded where a word will do. Buttons are one word (Quote · Approve · Keep · Revise · Send · Generate · Export); a label is a noun; a line of help is one clause with no personality. Rewrite every sentence of copy in the build longer than eight words to its shortest form; cut any line the screen already shows. Never "Just make it", "No questions", "the editor is the director's", "Show me on my film" or anything in that voice.
4. In Director mode every setting a person chooses, in any tab of any panel, is remembered on that node and on that step until changed. One Generate · N cr button, at the foot of the Inspector and on the bar, runs whatever is set; its price updates live as settings change. Nothing runs from a settings change alone.
5. Double-clicking anywhere on a canvas opens a node and the Inspector with it, on the tab that matters for that node. In Director mode on Elements the first thing the new node asks is its kind (see ELEMENTS); every setting that follows depends on it.
6. What a person selects never crowds the bar. The bar stays one line: the field, the mark, the price, the button. Questions, suggestions and Atomik's notes appear on a second line directly above the bar, the same width, dismissable, never inside it.
7. Agent mode never names an engine or a vendor, anywhere: not on the canvas, not in History, not on "How Particl works" outside its engine section. The new abilities (words that read, worlds, angles, moves, relighting, label fixes, colorways) are shown in Agent mode by what they do, never by who does them. Director mode names its engines in the Engine tab and nowhere else.
8. The top-left pill with the mark AND the wordmark is on every page at the same size. In v31.11 the wordmark is missing from the pill on production pages (Treatment shows the mark alone); put it back everywhere and keep it.
