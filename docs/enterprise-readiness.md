## Release rule

For each increment, review the final diff, pass type/lint and relevant automated checks, verify the isolated preview, merge with exact-head CI, then verify the new production deployment and runtime errors. Never promote a mock preview to production. Record implementation, tests, deployment and outstanding operational evidence separately. Do not call the platform fully production-proven while the remaining requirements are open.
