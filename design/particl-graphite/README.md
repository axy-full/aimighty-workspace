# Handoff: Particl Suites · Graphite — master design for `axy-full/aimighty-workspace`

Written 3 October 2026 against repo `main` (last read 2 October 2026; see `github.md`). Everything below describes `Particl Suites.dc.html`, the master design file. Open it in a browser with `support.js` beside it.

## Overview

Particl is the production studio in `axy-full/aimighty-workspace` (Next.js). The master design puts the whole product in one shell: a 56 px header with six destinations (Studio · Gen · Business · Viral · Atomik · Crew), a page strip per suite, a Library on the left, the stage in the middle, an Inspector on the right, plus ⌘K search, a Jobs tray, a right-click menu with undo, and toasts.

Built to high fidelity in this file:

- the shell, on every screen;
- Studio (Particl Production Studio), all ten stages;
- Gen (Generate): Video · Images · Audio in one composer, the model sheet, the Takes & assets wall, File to shot, Seedance Edit, Astra and Topaz upscales.

Specified but not yet designed (they show a dashed "This page is next in the rebuild" card): Atomik Agent (8 pages), Moleculr Business Suite (8 pages), Subatomik Viral Studio (3), Crew (3), Workspace (7 tabs), and the phone. Their IA, state scaffolding and rules are in this document under "Pending work"; the earlier Graphite files in `reference/` hold their content.

## About the design files

The `.dc.html` files are **design references built in HTML**: click-through prototypes of the intended look and behaviour, not production code. The task is to **recreate them inside the Next.js app** with its existing components (`components/graphite/*`, `components/ui/*`), its routes and its data, keeping every endpoint, role and price as the repo defines them. Do not ship the HTML. Treat `lib/shell/ia.ts` as the source of truth for suites, page names and order, and `CLAUDE.md § Pricing` as the only source of prices.

## Fidelity

**High-fidelity** for the shell, Studio and Gen: colours, type, spacing, radii, copy and interaction states are final; recreate them 1:1 with the codebase's primitives. Copy is verbatim from the repo's Graphite components where the repo has them (`components/graphite/production/*`, `GenView.tsx`, `ModelSheet.tsx`) and from `lib/shell/ia.ts` hints; where the repo had no prose yet the design's copy is the proposal.

**Spec only** for the pending suites: the pages exist in the strip with their final titles and hints, and the data model is scaffolded, but there is no layout to copy yet.

## Ground rules (owner decisions — apply them in code, not just in the UI)

1. **Graphite, unchanged.** Pure black canvas, flat hairline-separated panels, one blue accent, SF-style sans. No glass, blur or gradients (the only gradients are the two project-tile swatches and the avatar). The `flair` prop (header aurora) stays **off**. Add no colours beyond the token list below.
2. **Provider APIs and loginless MCP only** (owner's decision, 28 September 2026). Nothing may need a Higgsfield sign-in: no connected account, no Higgsfield CLI, no skill packs, no connected-account catalogue. Any Higgsfield-backed work runs on Particl's own API key.
3. **Every paid action shows its price on its button**, e.g. `Generate · 43 cr`, `Render plate · 3 cr`, `Build identity · 54 cr`. Prices come from the repo rate card. When the card has no row for an action the button reads **`quoted`** and the app fetches a quote before the run; it never invents a figure. Free local actions say `free` (`Mix on this device · free`, `Render master · free`).
4. **Nothing is spent without a gate.** Generate freezes the quoted price; tools require `Review … cost` before the run button enables; Atomik runs stop at an approval gate; held jobs show their price as the approve button.
5. **IA from `lib/shell/ia.ts`**: suites, page ids, labels, titles, hints and order below are copied from it. If the file changes, the strip follows the file.
