Particl design round: "the board, made easy". Work in "Particl Suites.dc.html", the Graphite master. The reference is an earlier Node studio screen: match its darkness and density.

WHAT PARTICL IS
A production studio for ad films and social content. A person brings a brief; Particl's agent, Atomik, asks what it needs, writes the plan, prices every step, waits for approval, makes the work and checks it. Every paid action shows its price, and nothing is spent without approval.

WHY THIS ROUND
The product was hard to use: six destinations and about 40 pages; many settings before anything happens; invented names (Rig, Astra, Genjutsu, Moleculr, Subatomik); red errors on a brand-new project; price words like "quoted"; small, faint text. This round turns Studio into one board per production, puts every suite on the same board, makes every feature reachable by the agent, and makes the whole product easier to use.

RULES FOR EVERY STEP
- Keep Graphite's character: black, flat, hairline-separated, one blue accent (#0A84FF), SF type with Geist as fallback, the existing radii and motion. No glass, blur or gradients (only project swatches and avatars). Leave the flair switch off. No new colours.
- Every price is a sample from the rate card (CLAUDE.md § Pricing). Never invent one; mark each in the README with its rate-card row. Show "N cr", "up to N cr" (a live estimate) or "free". Never the bare word "quoted". Hovering a price shows its dollar value (1 cr = $0.10).
- Only a person approves spending. Atomik prepares and explains every approval; the person taps to approve; Atomik never approves on its own.
- Nothing may need a Higgsfield sign-in.
- Every feature is agentic: anything a button does, Atomik can do at the same price through the same approval, and every screen lets you ask Atomik instead of clicking. Exceptions belong to people alone: approving spend, spend limits, Top up, approving a post and recording consent.
- Names in the UI: Studio; Ads (was Moleculr Business Suite); Social (was Subatomik Viral Studio); Board (was Rig); 3D blocking (was Astra); Motion transfer and Object swap (no "Genjutsu"); Crew review; Make (was Gen); Identity (no "Soul"); Atomik stays the agent's name.
- Sample names (ZigZag Films, Maison Aurel, Mira, Northline, Dune Studies) may stay; list them as placeholders in the README.
- One step at a time; stop after each and wait for the go-ahead.

EASE RULES (every screen)
1. One primary button per screen, and it is the next step.
2. Auto by default; engine, model, agent, effort, lens, light and look chosen for the person and shown as one line ("Seedance 2.5 · 1080p · 5 s · 43 cr · Change"); advanced settings folded in the Inspector.
3. Set once, carried everywhere: aspect, frame rate, look and cast from the brief.
4. Everything saves itself; Undo instead of "Are you sure?".
5. Plain words; icons always labelled; no two-letter tiles.
6. Every result is a card with Approve, Reject, Change with words, Use as reference, Versions.
7. Money without anxiety: one approval per plan listing every paid step, a fix allowance (at most two fixes per shot) and the total, admin steps marked; the balance against the total with Top up right there if short; a thinking-cost line before a request; "spend without asking" marked; failed steps say "Nothing billed" with Retry when nothing was charged.
8. Readable dark: ≥ 12 px and ≥ 55 % white; visible focus rings; ≥ 44 px targets on phones.
9. Every wait shows live progress, a time estimate and "Notify me when done".
10. Empty states teach by doing.
