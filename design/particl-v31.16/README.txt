WHAT IS FREE AND WHAT IS NOT
  Nothing intelligent is free: every generation, Options, Enhance (1 cr, the full prompt first), auditions, transcription (by the minute), reading a document (by pages; scans add OCR) or a reference film (by the minute), research, writing, planning, re-cuts, previews, captions. Free means nothing intelligent ran: the Preflight and the quote, your own typing and hand edits, copies, moving, Keep, undo, hand editing on the timeline, exports of what was already made and billed. Putting a file in is free; the moment Atomik has to read it, that is priced by how much it has to read, and the price appears on the Preflight before anything runs ("Campaign brief.docx · 14 pages · Reading · 4–9 cr"). A reference picture dropped as an element shows no price: nothing reads it until a take does. Prices read "N cr", "N–M cr", "up to N cr" or "free"; a credit is $0.10 and hovering a price shows its dollars.

THE LIBRARY HOLDS EVERYTHING
  Every take, frame, variant, audition, render, version, document and upload is in the Library from the moment it exists, in every production; Keep and un-keep change a mark, never where a thing lives. Beside the types a second row: Kept · Unkept · Everything. Unkept holds every take and frame that was not kept, by production, step and shot, the shot's number large, "Keep" on hover. The Trash is not the Library: it opens from the Library's "Trash · n", holds only what a person deleted, says so at its top, and Undo puts a thing back with its marks.
