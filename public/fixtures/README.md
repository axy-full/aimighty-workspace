# Engine fixtures

What the engines "return" when `ENGINE_MOCK=1` (development and tests). No vendor is called and nothing is billed; every render lands as one of these.

- `clip.mp4` — 10 seconds of Big Buck Bunny at 360p. © 2008 Blender Foundation, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via test-videos.co.uk.
- `tone.mp3` — a three-second sample tone from samplelib.com (free to use).
- `still.png` — a generated gradient.
